# Positioning, sources and next releases

## The problem to solve

A business is a coordinated set of promises, rights, resources, decisions and cash flows. A useful system helps people establish those things, then keeps them coherent as facts change. Producing documents or simulating an executive team is only part of that work.

Business Foundry explores a reusable operating foundation. A concept creates a workspace and a work plan. The same records support starting, acquiring and operating. An AI client can prepare work, but accountable people and actual evidence determine consequential completion. The public engine is separated from private business records.

This is an architectural/product thesis, not a proven market advantage or a claim to be the first such product.

## Adjacent tools

Existing tools already solve substantial parts of the problem. The integration opportunity is maintaining a consistent connection among facts, decisions, evidence, obligations and outcomes across them.

| Area | Examples to evaluate | Foundry's proposed relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Formation and company legal work | [Stripe Atlas](https://stripe.com/atlas), [Clerky](https://www.clerky.com/) | Prepare the factual brief and retain accepted provider results; do not recreate their legal/provider operations |
| Commercial agreement standards | [Common Paper](https://commonpaper.com/standards/) | Link a reviewed standard where applicable; preserve its license and attribution if later vendoring any template |
| Business planning and forecasting | [LivePlan](https://www.liveplan.com/) | Benchmark the usefulness of planning and scenario workflows; export clear assumptions |
| Operational ERP/accounting | [ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext) | Potential downstream system of record; avoid treating the v0.1 receipt register as a full ERP |
| Business valuation | [Valutico](https://www.valutico.com/) | Benchmark analytical workflows; licensed market data and professional valuation remain separate capabilities |
| Agent organization | [Paperclip](https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip) | Existing agent orchestration, roles, work and budgets; compare carefully before building overlapping execution machinery |
| Agent execution | [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/), provider APIs and compatible clients | Connect through scoped tools while preserving review authority in the business service |

These links describe adjacent categories, not an exhaustive current feature comparison, endorsement, partnership, price comparison or claim that any provider supports an available API integration. No live formation, banking, CRM, accounting, signature or valuation-data connector is included in v0.1.

## Primary sources for the planning guide

References reviewed on **5 November 2026**. A link check and a source review are not a legal applicability determination. Revalidate relevant requirements against the actual business and current source before execution.

- [SBA: launch your business](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/launch-your-business/) — location, structure, registration, tax IDs, permits, banking and insurance.
- [SBA: plan your business](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/) — market research, business plans, startup costs, funding and buying a business.
- [GOV.UK: set up a private limited company](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation) — company formation and continuing director/record/reporting responsibilities.
- [Verksamt: starting and registering a limited company](https://verksamt.se/en/setting-up/choose-business-type/limited-company) — Swedish formation, capital, registration and tax setup considerations.
- [Common Paper: standard contracts](https://commonpaper.com/standards/) — external agreement standards. This repository links to them and does not reproduce their contract text. Their licensing is separate from this repository's MIT license.
- [OpenAI: Structured Outputs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs) — optional validated AI output format; account/model support must be tested in the deployment.
- [Official MCP Python SDK](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/python-sdk) — scoped AI-client interface using the pinned SDK.

The current jurisdiction packages are high-level prompts, not full rule engines. They intentionally avoid declaring that a particular legal form, registration, tax election or reporting exception is correct without the facts and a current review.

## Release sequence

| Stage | Concrete acceptance condition |
|---|---|
| v0.1 local foundation | Fresh install; concept-to-cash synthetic flow; startup and acquisition models; evidence and authority checks; backup/restore; passing API/browser checks |
| Private Polymer pilot | One paid, accepted delivery with reviewed rights, relevant setup, reconciled receipt and comparison with prior economics |
| Repeatable service operation | Multiple actual deliveries, observed support/renewal obligations, validated correction and financial-reversal flows, required accounting integration |
| Provider integrations | Sandbox-tested connectors with credential isolation, explicit action authority, idempotency, status reconciliation and independent provider receipts |
| Deeper acquisition modeling | Independently reviewed historical reconciliation, working-capital schedules, debt tranches, earnouts, exit/IRR sensitivities and integration actuals |
| Larger organizations | SSO/MFA, separation of duties, policy-defined monetary authority, independently protected audit history, durable queues, migration strategy and load/security testing |
| Additional jurisdictions | Maintained source contracts, a named competent reviewer, versioned applicability rules, effective dates and regression examples |

A large organization is not a larger number of AI role labels. It requires real delegated authority, reliable records, controls, operational capacity and evidence that its promises are being fulfilled. Each stage should be earned through observed use.
